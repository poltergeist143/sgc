Who We Are

Rooted in Faith, Anchored in Grace

Our faith is founded upon the Word of God, our salvation rests upon the finished work of Jesus Christ, and our hope is in His glorious return.

A Sanctuary of Healing, Fellowship, and Salvation

Whether you are taking your first steps in seeking God or looking for a church family to call home, there is a warm seat and an open heart waiting for you here.

Founded in 1986 through the missionary work of Reverend Peter Ng, Saved By Grace Church began with evangelism and outreach among underserved communities in Pasil, Cebu City. He established the International School of Missions (ISM), training students to share the Gospel and disciple believers. The growing ministry became Jesus Saves Mission, initially gathering at Ahok Building, Suba, Cebu City, under Pastors Melendez and Othello Caturan.

In April 2001, the church became independent under Pastor Renen Candia. Leadership was entrusted to Pastor Joel M. Tatoy in 2002, who was ordained in 2004. In 2003, the church adopted the name Saved By Grace Fundamental Bible Church, later shortened to Saved By Grace Church, founded on Ephesians 2:8–9.

Our Foundation

Our Statement of Faith

A Biblical Declaration of Our Doctrinal Beliefs


Saved by Grace Church believes that the Holy Bible is the inspired and preserved Word of God and the final authority for all matters of faith, doctrine, and Christian practice. We hold to the fundamental doctrines of the Christian faith and affirm the following Statement of Faith:

The Holy Scriptures

We believe that the Holy Bible, consisting of the 66 books of the Old and New Testaments, is the verbally inspired and preserved Word of God. It is without error in its original writings and is the final authority for faith, doctrine, and Christian living.

The Godhead

We believe in one true and living God, eternally existing in three distinct Persons: God the Father, God the Son, and God the Holy Ghost. These three are one in essence, equal in divine nature, and possess all the attributes of Deity.

THE DEITY AND PERSON OF JESUS CHRIST

We believe that Jesus Christ is the eternal Son of God, fully God and fully man. He was conceived by the Holy Ghost, born of the virgin Mary, lived a sinless life, performed miracles, died a substitutionary death for our sins, was buried, rose bodily from the dead, ascended into heaven, and now serves as our High Priest and Advocate.

THE HOLY SPIRIT

We believe that the Holy Spirit is God, the third Person of the Godhead. He convicts the world of sin, righteousness, and judgment; regenerates sinners; indwells believers; seals them unto the day of redemption; and empowers them for Christian living and service.

THE CREATION AND FALL OF MAN

We believe that God created man directly and specially in His own image. Man was created without sin but voluntarily disobeyed God and fell into sin, bringing spiritual death and condemnation upon humanity.

SALVATION BY GRACE THROUGH FAITH

We believe that salvation is entirely by God's grace through faith in the finished work of Jesus Christ. It is not earned through good works, religious ceremonies, human merit, or personal righteousness.

REPENTANCE AND FAITH

We believe that biblical repentance involves a change of mind concerning sin, self, and God, leading a sinner to turn to God in faith. Repentance and faith are inseparable aspects of conversion.

ETERNAL SECURITY OF THE BELIEVER

We believe that every genuinely saved believer is eternally secure in Jesus Christ. Salvation is God's gift, and the believer is sealed by the Holy Spirit unto the day of redemption.

THE CHURCH

We believe that the local New Testament church is a congregation of baptized believers who have voluntarily covenanted together for worship, fellowship, evangelism, discipleship, observance of the ordinances, and the preaching and teaching of God's Word.

THE ORDINANCES OF THE CHURCH

We believe that the Lord Jesus Christ instituted two ordinances for the local church: a. Water Baptism b. The Lord's Supper.

THE PRIESTHOOD OF BELIEVERS

We believe that every believer has direct access to God through Jesus Christ, our only Mediator and High Priest. No human priest or religious institution is necessary to mediate between God and the believer.

THE SEPARATION OF CHURCH AND STATE

We believe that the church and civil government have distinct God-ordained responsibilities. The church must remain free to worship, preach, and fulfill the Great Commission without governmental control, while believers are instructed to respect lawful authorities.

CHRISTIAN SEPARATION

We believe that Christians are called to live holy and separated lives, rejecting worldliness, sinful practices, false doctrine, and compromise with unbelief.

EVANGELISM AND MISSIONS

We believe that every Christian has the responsibility to proclaim the Gospel of Jesus Christ to the lost. The Great Commission commands believers to preach the Gospel, make disciples, baptize believers, and teach them to observe Christ's commandments.

THE SECOND COMING OF CHRIST

We believe in the personal, visible, and bodily return of Jesus Christ. We believe in the blessed hope of His coming, the resurrection of the dead, the judgment of the wicked, and the establishment of His righteous kingdom according to God's prophetic program.

THE RESURRECTION AND FINAL DESTINY

We believe in the bodily resurrection of all humanity: the saved unto eternal life and the lost unto everlasting condemnation. We believe that heaven is the eternal dwelling place of the redeemed and that hell is the place of everlasting punishment for those who reject God's salvation.

THE BELIEVER'S REWARDS

We believe that believers will stand before the Judgment Seat of Christ, not to determine their eternal salvation, but to give an account of their Christian service and receive rewards according to their works.
