Who We Are

Rooted in Faith, Anchored in Grace

Saved by Grace Church is a Christ-exalting, Bible-believing spiritual home dedicated to proclaiming the uncompromised Gospel and loving people into fullness of life.

A Sanctuary of Healing, Fellowship, and Salvation

Whether you are taking your first steps in seeking God or looking for a church family to call home, there is a warm seat and an open heart waiting for you here.

Established in 1998 from a small living-room prayer gathering of seven faithful believers, Saved by Grace Church has blossomed into a thriving multi-generational assembly. Our heartbeat is simple yet eternal: to glorify Jesus Christ, stand steadfast upon the Word of God, and minister the boundless grace that redeems every broken heart.

Here, we celebrate that salvation is not earned by human striving, but freely received through faith in our Lord and Savior Jesus Christ. We are ordinary people experiencing God’s extraordinary love.

Our Foundation

Our Statement of Faith

The timeless biblical truths upon which our faith, doctrine, and church family stand.

The Scriptures

We believe the Holy Bible is the divinely inspired, infallible, and authoritative Word of God, providing our complete guide for faith and Christian living.

The Triune God

We worship one true God eternally existent in three distinct co-equal persons: the Father, the Son Jesus Christ, and the Holy Spirit.

Salvation by Grace

Salvation is the free gift of God given to undeserving sinners. We are justified solely through repentance and faith in Jesus Christ’s finished cross-work.

The Church & Baptism

The universal body of Christ consists of all true believers, practicing believer’s baptism by immersion and frequent communion in remembrance of Christ.

The Great Commission

Every disciple is commissioned to proclaim the Gospel to all nations, serve the vulnerable, and lead others into devoted spiritual maturity.

The Blessed Hope

We await the triumphant, personal return of Jesus Christ, the resurrection of believers unto eternal joy, and the renewal of all creation.
